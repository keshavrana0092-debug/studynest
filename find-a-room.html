Our services for students

More than listings: StudyNest supports you from your first search to your first week in Melbourne.

Live video inspections

See the room, the kitchen and the street on a live call with the host before you commit, at a time that suits your time zone.

AI renting assistant

Ask questions like “What is a bond?” or “Are bills included?” and get clear answers instantly, 24/7.

Roommate matching

Tell us about your study habits, sleep schedule and lifestyle, and we suggest share houses with compatible housemates.

Protected payments

Your first payment is held securely and released to the host only after you move in.

Suburb guides

Compare suburbs by transport, typical rents, shops and student life before you choose.

Arrival checklist

A personalised checklist for your first week: keys, bond lodgement, transport card and setting up bills.
