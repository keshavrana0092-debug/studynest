About StudyNest

We help international students find a safe, verified home in Melbourne before they arrive, so they can focus on their studies, not on rental stress.

Why we started

Moving to another country to study is exciting, but finding somewhere to live is often the hardest part. Most international students have to secure housing from overseas, without seeing the room, knowing the suburbs or understanding Australian rental rules.

Many rely on social media groups and general listing sites, where fake listings and deposit scams are a real risk. StudyNest was created to make the first step of student life in Melbourne safe and simple.

Our mission

To give every international student a safe, affordable and verified home near their campus, booked with confidence before they land.

How we verify every host and room

Trust is the core of StudyNest. Each listing goes through four checks before it is published.

CHECK 1

Identity

Hosts confirm their identity and their right to rent out the property.

CHECK 2

Video walkthrough

Our team views the room on a live video call to confirm it matches the photos.

CHECK 3

Clear terms

Rent, bills, bond and house rules are written clearly on every listing.

CHECK 4

Protected payment

Payments are held until the student moves in and confirms the room.

What we value

Safety first

We would rather list fewer rooms than list one we cannot verify.

Plain language

Renting rules explained simply, for students who are new to Australia.

Fair for hosts too

Hosts get reliable, screened tenants and simple tools to manage their rooms.

Who we help

International students starting or continuing study in Melbourne, especially those arriving in the next one to six months.

Hosts, including homeowners with spare rooms and property managers, who want long-term student tenants.

Parents and families who want to know their child is moving into a safe, verified home.

About this project

StudyNest is a startup concept developed as a working prototype for the BUSN9155 Digital Marketing and Analytics unit. Room listings shown on this site are sample data used to demonstrate how the platform works.
