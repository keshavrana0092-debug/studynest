Frequently asked questions

Can I book a room before I arrive in Australia?

Yes. StudyNest is designed for students who are still overseas. You can take a live video inspection with the host, ask questions, and secure the room before your flight.

How does StudyNest check hosts and listings?

Every host's identity is checked and every listing is confirmed through a video walkthrough before it goes live. Listings that pass display a Verified host badge.

When do I pay, and is my money safe?

Your first payment is held securely and only released to the host after you move in and confirm the room matches the listing.

Which universities do you cover?

StudyNest focuses on Melbourne, including the University of Melbourne, RMIT, Monash (Clayton), Deakin (Burwood), Swinburne and La Trobe, with more campuses to come.
