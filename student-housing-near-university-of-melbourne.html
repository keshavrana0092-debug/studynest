Best suburbs for students near the University of Melbourne

Each suburb suits a different budget and lifestyle. Use this guide to shortlist where to search.

Carlton

Walking distance to campus, with cafes, libraries and parks. Popular and in high demand, so expect higher rents.

Parkville

Right next to the university and quieter than Carlton. Good for students who want to walk to class.

Brunswick

A lively area with many share houses and often lower rents, connected to campus by tram.

North Melbourne

Close to the CBD and campus, with a mix of apartments and older terrace share houses.

Tips for renting near UniMelb

  • Start early. Demand peaks before Semester 1 and Semester 2.
  • Check travel time, not just distance. A room on a direct tram line can beat a closer one.
  • Ask what bills include. Electricity, gas, water and internet can add a lot to weekly costs.
  • Never pay before verifying. Read our guide to avoiding rental scams.

Questions students ask

Which suburbs are closest to the University of Melbourne?

Carlton and Parkville are right next to the main campus. North Melbourne, Brunswick and Fitzroy are also popular with students and are a short tram or bike ride away.

Is it better to live in a share house or a studio?

A room in a share house usually costs less and helps you meet people, while a studio gives you more privacy at a higher price. Many first-year international students start in a share house or homestay.

How early should I start looking for a room?

Start searching two to three months before your semester begins. Rooms near campus are in high demand before each intake, so booking early gives you more choice.
